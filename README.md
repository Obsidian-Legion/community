# Obsidian Legion community

A home for independent programmers, systems engineers, and system architects to exchange ideas, find collaborators, and turn useful ideas into working projects.

[Website](https://wolfbreed1829.github.io/obsidian-legion/) · [Discussions](https://github.com/obsidian-legion/community/discussions) · [Propose a build](https://github.com/obsidian-legion/community/issues/new?template=build-idea.yml)

## Take your first step

1. Introduce yourself in Discussions: what you build, what you are learning, and what you want to make next.
2. Ask a specific question, share something you made, or help another builder.
3. When an idea is ready for work, open a build proposal with a small, achievable first milestone.

## How we organize

**Discussions** are for conversations, questions, learning, and showing your work.

**Issues** describe proposed builds and concrete work. A proposal is an invitation to discuss scope; it does not commit anyone to building or maintaining it.

**Repositories** hold individual projects as they take shape. Each project should explain how to run it, contribute, and report problems.

**Teams** bring together programmers, systems engineers, and system architects. The organization owner manages membership and repository access. You can participate in public discussions without becoming an organization member.

## Community expectations

- Respect people and critique the work constructively.
- Explain tradeoffs and share enough context for others to reproduce your results.
- Credit sources and respect the licenses of code and assets you reuse.
- Keep secrets, credentials, and private personal information out of public posts.
- Build and test only on systems you own or have permission to use.
- Avoid spam, harassment, and misleading claims about a project's capabilities.

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before opening a proposal or pull request.
